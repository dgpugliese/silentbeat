Common questions.

Does upgrading change the security model?

No. Free and premium accounts use the same split-key crypto, the same passkey-PRF recipient flow, and the same signed public log. Premium just lifts numerical limits.

What happens if my subscription lapses?

Existing armed switches keep running. You can still check in, defuse, and let them release normally. You just can't create new switches that exceed the free-tier limits, and you'll be capped to one active switch.

Is my payment data on your servers?

No. Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy as the merchant of record. We see your subscription status (active/cancelled/expired), never your card details.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Manage your subscription from the settings page; cancellation is immediate (no auto-renewal) but premium privileges last through the period you've already paid for.
