SilentBeat is a digital safety net. If the person who invited you stops checking in, a message they wrote will be released to you. To accept, your phone or laptop will set up a passkey — that's the key SilentBeat uses to deliver the message safely later. Nothing is sent yet.

// what happens when you tap accept
  1. Your device asks you to set up a passkey (Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, or a security key).
  2. SilentBeat stores nothing about you that can be unlocked without that passkey.
  3. If your contact ever goes silent past their timer, the message arrives in your inbox with a one-tap link to read it.

If this seems wrong or unexpected, just close this tab. Nothing happens until you tap accept. Read more about how SilentBeat works.
